Holonomic Mobile Bases

Designed and built three holonomic platforms: a four-wheel mecanum base, a four-wheel omni base, and a compact three-wheel omni base for constrained spaces. Each platform combines encoder feedback with PID control and supports joystick or RC operation. Solved the kinematic and control challenges of the three-wheel architecture to achieve precise 360° mobility, carrying each system from mechanical design through closed-loop motion control.
