Multi-Robot SLAM for Large-Scale Building Mapping

Field deployment media coming soon

Designed a standardized payload integrating stereo cameras, Ouster LiDAR, VectorNav IMU, and u-blox RTK GPS, then deployed it across Boston Dynamics Spot, Unitree Go2 and Go2-Wheeled robots, and AgileX UGVs.

Coordinated role-specific mapping across two seven-floor buildings—Spot on staircases, UGVs in laboratories, and Unitree robots in corridors—and merged the segmented datasets into complete 3D reconstructions. Implemented FAST-LIO for drift-resistant LiDAR–inertial mapping in large-scale, GPS-denied environments.
