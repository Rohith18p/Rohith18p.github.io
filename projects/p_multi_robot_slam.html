Multi-Robot SLAM for Large-Scale Building Mapping

Designed and deployed a standardized payload—stereo cameras, Ouster LiDAR, VectorNav IMU, and u-blox RTK GPS—across five heterogeneous robots: Boston Dynamics Spot, Unitree Go2/Go2-Wheels, and AgileX UGVs.

Built the C++/roscpp acquisition stack to synchronize four asynchronous streams on a common clock while sustaining full-rate LiDAR and stereo capture without dropped frames. Used FAST-LIO to map two seven-floor GPS-denied buildings, then merged per-robot segments into a drift-resistant 3D reconstruction.
