Graduate Researcher

Robotics Research Center (IIIT-Hyderabad)

May 2022 – Jul 2024

  • Implemented interactive imitation learning for in-hand Rubik’s Cube manipulation using 11-DOF finger proprioception and continuous cube-layer angular feedback, deploying learned 90° layer-rotation policies on the physical bionic hand.
  • Engineered multiple bionic arm prototypes using diverse architectures, including under-actuated (tendon-driven), fully actuated, and fully dexterous systems.
  • Developed novel mechanical architectures for wrist articulation and proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joints, enhancing dexterity and functional range of robotic fingers.
  • Designed and prototyped a bipedal robot with anthropomorphic proportions and actuation strategies closely emulating human biomechanics.
  • Gained deep expertise in rapid prototyping through extensive use of FDM, SLA, and SLS 3D printing technologies for high-fidelity robotic components.
