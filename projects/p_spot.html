SLAM with Spot

Built an indoor 3D mapping pipeline around Boston Dynamics Spot using its five stereo cameras, a Velodyne LiDAR, and onboard odometry. Configured a Jetson Orin Nano through Spot’s payload interface for data collection and remote SSH operation with custom ROS launch files. Processed the recorded datasets with RTAB-Map in ROS to reconstruct detailed 3D point clouds across multiple environments.
