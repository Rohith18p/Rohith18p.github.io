Contingency MPPI

Implemented Contingency-MPPI, embedding a backup plan inside an MPPI controller so every optimization step produces both a nominal trajectory and a route to a safe region. Extended the framework with occlusion-aware safe-zone detection, allowing the robot to identify refuge online instead of relying on predefined locations. Simulation and hardware validation with LiDAR–inertial localization demonstrated real-time, risk-aware planning under uncertainty.
