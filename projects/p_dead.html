Dead Reckoning

Built a ROS 2 dead-reckoning pipeline for vehicle trajectory estimation using custom drivers for a GPS puck and VectorNav VN-100 IMU. Characterized GPS behavior through stationary and linear-motion tests and quantified IMU noise and drift with Allan variance. Corrected magnetometer errors from circular-drive calibration data, then fused GPS and IMU measurements with a complementary filter during street trials to estimate heading, velocity, and position.
