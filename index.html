01 — About

Building robots that
think, sense, and act.

I'm a robotics researcher focused on developing autonomy stacks that enable robots to act intelligently and adaptively in the real world. My path into robotics began with building — mobile robots, manipulators, bionic hands, and competition-ready platforms. These early experiences laid the foundation for deeper research into robot learning, real-time planning, SLAM, and perception.

Currently at the Autonomy and Intelligence Lab at Northeastern University, I work on risk-aware trajectory planning and safety-guaranteed control strategies with contingency planning. My work emphasises real-world deployment and robustness under uncertainty, combining ROS2 and hardware-in-the-loop systems.
